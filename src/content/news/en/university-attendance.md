## The problem

Paper registers took time from every lecture and were easy to fake. Students signed in for friends who were not there, and exam eligibility had to be worked out by hand at the end of every term.

## What we automated

Students now check in by scanning a QR code that changes every 15 seconds, so a photo of the code shared in a group chat no longer works. Attendance, exam eligibility, reports and semester setup now run themselves.

## The result

- Roll call in seconds
- Proxy sign-ins blocked
- Eligibility lists in one click
- A new semester set up in an hour
