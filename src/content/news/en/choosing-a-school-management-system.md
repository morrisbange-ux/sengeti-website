A school management system should save the office hours every week, help teachers spend less time on paperwork and keep parents informed. Choose the wrong one and it becomes another login nobody uses. These are the questions we suggest every head, bursar and board asks before signing anything, whichever supplier they choose.

## 1. Does it work for parents who have little or no data?

Most parents will meet the system on a phone, often with a small bundle. Ask how big the pages are, whether parents can get alerts by SMS, and whether they can check a balance or an absence without opening an app. If the answer depends on fast internet, many families will simply never see it.

## 2. Can teachers take the register offline?

Connections drop and load-shedding happens in the middle of lessons. A good system lets a teacher mark the register on a phone without a signal and sends it when the connection returns. Ask to see it done with mobile data switched off.

## 3. How are fees, receipts and arrears handled?

Fees are where most schools feel the pain. Check that the system issues invoices and receipts, tracks who owes what, handles sponsors and bursaries, and lets parents pay from home with the payment methods they already use, such as EcoCash or a card. Ask how a parent sees their balance.

## 4. Does it fit the Zimbabwean curriculum and calendar?

Terms, forms and grades, ZIMSEC candidate lists, O-level and A-level subject choices, and Ministry returns should be built in, not worked around. A system designed for another country's school year will cost the office time every term.

## 5. Will it look like our school?

Parents trust what carries your school's name. Ask whether your crest and colours appear on the sign-in page, the app, receipts and reports, and whether messages arrive from your school rather than the supplier.

## 6. Who owns our data, and can we take it with us?

Your learners' records belong to your school. Ask where the data is kept, how often it is backed up, who at the supplier can see it, and whether the head can download everything at any time. Read the supplier's privacy policy before you sign.

## 7. How long does it take to go live, and who trains our staff?

Software that nobody is trained to use is wasted money. Ask for a week-by-week plan, who trains the head and office, the teachers and the parents, and whether training happens in person.

## 8. What does it really cost?

Ask for the full price: setup, training, licences, SMS, hosting, updates and support. A per-learner price per term is easy to budget, but make sure nothing important is charged as an extra later.
