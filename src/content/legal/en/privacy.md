This policy explains how Sengeti Technologies ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you use this website and our products, including the SengetiEdu web and phone apps. We handle personal information in line with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]. Where we serve organisations in other countries, we also follow the data protection laws that apply there.

## Who is responsible for your information

For this website and enquiries you send us, Sengeti Technologies is responsible for your personal information.

For SengetiEdu, the school, college, university or organisation that uses it decides what information is collected about its learners, students, parents and staff and why. We process that information on the institution's behalf and only on its instructions. Questions about records held by an institution should go to that institution first.

## Information we collect

**When you contact us:** your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and the content of your message.

**When you use SengetiEdu:**

- Account details such as name, phone number, email address, learner, student or staff number, role, class and school.
- Parents' and guardians' contact details, and how they prefer to receive messages.
- Attendance records: register entries, check-in times, class and date.
- Selfie photos and location checks, when an institution switches them on, to confirm the right person checked in.
- Schoolwork and results: homework, quizzes, marks, report cards and comments.
- Fees and payments: invoices, receipts and balances. Card and EcoCash payments are processed by the payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
- Notices and messages sent through the app, SMS or WhatsApp, and questions asked to the assistant.
- Where a school uses those modules: who may collect a child, transport routes, boarding records, and sick bay notes such as allergies.
- Technical information such as device type, app version and error logs, used to keep the app working.

**When you browse this website:** basic technical information that web servers record, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you use the chat assistant, your questions are answered in your browser and are not sent to us unless you choose to contact us.

## Camera access

The Android app asks for camera access only to scan check-in QR codes and to take check-in selfies. It does not use the camera at any other time.

## How we use information

- To provide, secure and support our products.
- To reply to enquiries and requests for demos or quotes.
- To improve our software and fix problems.
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run our products, such as cloud hosting, email, SMS and WhatsApp delivery, payment processing, the AI service that powers the SengetiEdu assistant, and the form service that delivers contact messages. They may use it only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

## Where information is stored

Information is stored on secure cloud servers, which may be located outside Zimbabwe. Where that happens we take steps to make sure it stays protected to the standard this policy describes.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Schools and institutions using SengetiEdu decide how long their records are kept, and their data is deleted or returned when they stop using the service.

## Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), two-step sign-in, access controls and regular backups to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will act quickly if a problem occurs.

## Children

SengetiEdu is used by junior schools (from ECD), senior schools, colleges and universities, so it holds information about children. That information is collected by and on behalf of the school, which is responsible for any consent required from parents or guardians. Each school's information is kept private and separate from every other school's, and the head can download it at any time.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. If your information was provided by an institution, we will work with that institution to respond. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Authority (POTRAZ) in Zimbabwe.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
