This policy explains how Sengeti Technologies ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you use this website and our products, including the Sengeti Attendance web and Android apps. We handle personal information in line with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07].

## Who is responsible for your information

For this website and enquiries you send us, Sengeti Technologies is responsible for your personal information.

For Sengeti Attendance, the school or organisation that uses the app decides what information is collected about its students and staff and why. We process that information on the school's behalf and only on its instructions. Questions about records held by a school should go to that school first.

## Information we collect

**When you contact us:** your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and the content of your message.

**When you use Sengeti Attendance:**

- Account details such as name, email address, role and school.
- Attendance records: check-in times, class and date.
- Selfie photos, when a school uses selfie check-in, to confirm the right person checked in.
- Marks entered into the gradebook.
- Technical information such as device type, app version and error logs, used to keep the app working.

**When you browse this website:** basic technical information that web servers record, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Camera access

The Android app asks for camera access only to scan check-in QR codes and to take check-in selfies. It does not use the camera at any other time.

## How we use information

- To provide, secure and support our products.
- To reply to enquiries and requests for demos or quotes.
- To improve our software and fix problems.
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run our products, such as cloud hosting, email and the form service that delivers contact messages. They may use it only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

## Where information is stored

Information is stored on secure cloud servers, which may be located outside Zimbabwe. Where that happens we take steps to make sure it stays protected to the standard this policy describes.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Schools using Sengeti Attendance decide how long their records are kept, and their data is deleted or returned when they stop using the service.

## Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular backups to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will act quickly if a problem occurs.

## Children

Sengeti Attendance is used by schools to record student attendance. Student information is collected by and on behalf of the school, which is responsible for any consent required from parents or guardians.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. If your information was provided by a school, we will work with that school to respond. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Authority (POTRAZ) in Zimbabwe.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
