Paper registers take time from every lecture, are easy to fake, and leave exam eligibility to be worked out by hand at the end of the semester. Sengeti Attendance replaces them with a fast, reliable digital system.

Students check in from their own phones in seconds. Lecturers and administrators always know who attended, and eligibility, reports and semester setup run themselves. It works on everyday Android phones and is built to cope with slow connections.
